# Client confirmations required before production

These items remain intentionally unasserted. Their absence does not block the Vercel review deployment.

| Item | Current treatment | Required confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact form delivery | Disabled; phone and email are the only live contact channels | Form provider, destination endpoint, spam protection, retention policy and test delivery |
| Google Analytics and Google Ads | Consent-ready code only; no identifiers are committed | GA4 measurement ID, Google Ads ID, conversion labels, consent/legal approval and conversion definitions |
| Google Search Console | No verification token is committed | Production property and verification method |
| Privacy and terms | Informational review copy | Attorney/client-approved production language, company legal name and jurisdiction |
| Northeast operations | Described only as individually reviewed published markets | Exact active locations, service terms and approval to market them |
| Fleet imagery and specifications | Category imagery and conservative stated capacity | Client-approved fleet photos, vehicle models, capacities and luggage guidance |
| Reviews | Dated static snapshot and link to current Google profile | Authorized review source, refresh cadence and approval to display additional testimonials |
| Memberships/affiliations | Not claimed or displayed | Documentary proof and approved logo/licensing terms if applicable |
| Production environment | Preview is non-indexable and canonicalizes to the production domain | Approved production domain, DNS, Vercel/Hostinger deployment path and environment values |
